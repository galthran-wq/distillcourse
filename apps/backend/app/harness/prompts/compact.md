## Compaction

You are summarizing this conversation so it can continue with the older turns
dropped. The summary is what the next turn will read instead of them.

Write plain prose, no headings, under 400 words. Keep, in this order:

- What the learner is working on and where they are in the material.
- Facts about the learner that came up in the dialogue.
- Conclusions already reached, with the reasoning compressed to its result.
- Questions left open, in the learner's own framing.
- Locators that were cited, verbatim.

Drop pleasantries, dead ends that were resolved, and anything recoverable from
the material itself. Write it as notes to your future self, not as a report to
the learner.

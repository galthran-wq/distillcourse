## ARC (L10)

- **Inherits:** From a-priori-as-analytic: logic and mathematics as convention — which raises 'which convention?'; from verification-principle: the criterion's failure and the self-refutation reply ($L_{n+1}$ about $L_n$); from frege-logicism and logical-constructions-and-acquaintance: the Aufbau's ancestry (abstraction by equivalence relations; constructions).
- **Contributes:** Carnap's mature position, the one Quine attacks: tolerance (choose a language, judge it by fruitfulness), explication (replace the vague by the exact, with criteria of adequacy), and the internal/external distinction (existence questions inside a framework are theoretical; the framework's adoption is practical) — a deflationary anti-metaphysics that survives the criterion's collapse, and the relative a priori.
- **Hands off:** To two-dogmas-analyticity (the framework/fact line *is* the analytic/synthetic line, Quine says), to holism-and-naturalism (Quine's 'ontology continuous with science' against external questions as practical), to simultaneity-and-operational-definition (coordinative definitions and the relative a priori), to lewis-possible-worlds (Lewis's modal realism as Quine's method answering a Carnapian external question).

Spine step: as for verification-principle ("… logic is a choice of framework …").

## Explanation plan

1. **The problem.** Two disputes that look substantive and go nowhere: intuitionist vs classical logician ('you are doing logic wrong'); platonist vs nominalist ('do numbers really exist?') while both affirm 'there is a prime between 10 and 20'. The criterion of the verification lesson calls the metaphysical sentence meaningless and stops; it does not say what the disputants are doing or how a working mathematician should proceed. Sub-problem: can the disputes be *re-described* so that each side's claim becomes a sensible one?
2. **Tolerance.** Logical Syntax §17: 'it is not our business to set up prohibitions, but to arrive at conventions'; everyone may build his own logic, provided he states his rules. The intuitionist and the classicist have chosen different languages; neither is wrong; the choice is judged by consistency and fruitfulness. The because: once logic is convention (previous lesson), the question 'which logic is true?' has no answer and 'which is useful for what?' does. The math reader's hook: axiom pluralism (Euclidean/non-Euclidean, ZF/ZFC) is this principle in daily use.
3. **Explication.** Philosophy's job in a framework: replace a vague ordinary concept (explicandum) by an exact one (explicatum) satisfying criteria of adequacy — similarity, exactness, fruitfulness, simplicity; warmth → temperature; 'probability' → two explicata; 'analytic' → 'analytic-in-$L$'. Why this is not definition: the explicatum need not preserve the old usage, only serve the old purpose. The Aufbau's quasi-analysis as explication's ancestor: Frege's abstraction by an equivalence relation (Grundlagen §62 — `<Ref>` to logicism) used to construct qualities from experience (one paragraph; the Aufbau's failure is Quine's lesson).
4. **Internal and external questions (1950).** Inside the number framework, 'is there a prime between 10 and 20?' has a theoretical answer; 'do numbers exist?' asked *of* the framework is not a theoretical question at all but a practical one — whether to adopt the framework — answered by expediency. The same for the thing language ('do physical objects exist?') and for electrons. The because: the framework supplies the meaning of 'exist' for its entities; outside it the word has no rules. Realism vs instrumentalism about electrons becomes a dispute about the usefulness of a language, which is why Feigl, Frank and Carnap could disagree without a fact dividing them (Uebel §3.4).
5. **The relative a priori.** Within a framework, its rules are a priori (not revisable by experience within it); across frameworks, they are revisable by choice. Reichenbach's constitutive a priori (relativity); Carnap's 'logic of science'; Neurath: 'that reason changes its a prioris is a matter for the history of the sciences'. Kuhn's Structure appearing in Carnap's own Encyclopedia (1962) and Carnap agreeing — the irony stated dryly (Uebel §3.7).
6. **Why it mattered / what refuted or survived it.** Mattered: an anti-metaphysics that does not need the criterion — the deflationary programme that outlived verificationism. Refuted: Quine (next module) — the internal/external line is the analytic/synthetic line, and if that line cannot be drawn, framework choice and fact are continuous ('ontology is continuous with science'); and Lewis's later realism about worlds is exactly Quine's method applied where Carnap would have seen a practical choice. Survived: explication as the method philosophers use when they define anything (conceptual engineering, Uebel §4); the pragmatics of theory choice; the 'science of possibilities'.

## Pitfalls / subtleties to flag

- Carnap's texts are not on disk: every quotation is second-hand until staged; mark the References accordingly and keep direct quotation to the two sentences Uebel gives.
- 'Framework' is not 'theory': a framework is a language with rules of formation and evaluation; a theory is stated in it. Keep the distinction or Quine's objection will be unintelligible.
- Do not make Carnap a relativist: tolerance is about *languages*, and consistency is objective; fruitfulness is judged by results.
- The Aufbau is one paragraph; its failure ('is at') belongs to holism-and-naturalism.
- 'Deflationary' must be defined at first use (the dispute dissolves rather than being answered).

## EXERCISE PLAN

1. (warm-up) Sort as internal or external, naming the framework: 'is there a largest prime?'; 'are there numbers?'; 'is there a planet beyond Neptune?'; 'are there physical objects?'; 'are there possible worlds?'.
2. (refute) "The principle of tolerance makes logic a matter of taste." Break it: state the two objective constraints (consistency; fruitfulness for stated purposes) and give a case where tolerance *rules out* a proposed logic.
3. (derivation) Write the criteria of adequacy for an explication and apply them to replacing 'the same temperature' by 'the same reading on a mercury thermometer'; find the criterion it fails (the thermometer's range) and say what a better explicatum must add.
4. (boundary probe) Take 'do electrons exist?' as internal to physics: what is its answer and by what rule? Then as external: what is the practical question? Then show what happens to the realist/instrumentalist dispute between Feigl and Frank — and say which fact, if any, could settle it.
5. (claim–evidence) Kuhn's paradigms and Carnap's frameworks: list two points of agreement (a priori within, change across) and one of difference (Kuhn's incommensurability vs Carnap's translatability), using Uebel's account; then judge whether the irony of Kuhn appearing in the Encyclopedia is an irony at all.
6. (FORWARD → two-dogmas-analyticity) On the internal/external view, 'there is a prime between 10 and 20' is true by the rules of the framework plus a calculation, and 'adopt the number framework' is a decision. Write down what sentence of the framework is *analytic* on this view and what makes it so; then ask what distinguishes 'true by the framework's rules' from 'true by convention plus fact'. The solution says Quine's whole attack is that this distinction is the analytic/synthetic distinction and cannot be drawn, with a `<Ref>`.
7. (research-shaped) Quasi-analysis constructs qualities as equivalence classes of experiences under 'similarity'. Show that if similarity is not transitive (two indistinguishable shades, each indistinguishable from a third, the outer two distinguishable), the classes overlap and the construction fails; state in one sentence why this is the same problem as Ayer's indirect resemblance (the constructions lesson), and what Carnap's 'imperfect community' difficulty names.

## APPLICATION spec (L16)

- **Artefact:** Einstein, 'Zur Elektrodynamik bewegter Körper' (1905), §1 'Definition der Gleichzeitigkeit': clocks at A and B are synchronous *by definition* if the light signal leaving A at $t_A$, reflected at B at $t_B$, returns at $t'_A$ with $t_B - t_A = t'_A - t_B$ (the $\varepsilon = \tfrac12$ convention, as Reichenbach later called it); the 1916 popular exposition's train and embankment (Ch. 9) for the relativity of simultaneity. Secondary artefact: the Schlick/Reichenbach reading (coordinative definitions, 1920–1928) and its modern critique.
- **Module results it uses:** the verification criterion (a concept defined by the procedure that decides it); the a-priori-as-analytic thesis (which geometry fits space is empirical; relativity as the refutation of Kant's cell); frameworks and the relative a priori (the synchrony rule as a framework choice).
- **Where the artefact departs from the idealisation:** (1) Einstein's definition is not a reduction of 'simultaneous' to observations; it is a stipulation *inside* a theory whose other postulate (light's constancy) is empirical, and the stipulation is constrained by that theory — the standard synchrony is the unique one definable from the light-cone structure alone (Malament 1977), so 'free convention' overstates it; (2) the coordinative-definition reading (Schlick, Reichenbach) of general relativity is faulted by Ryckman, DiSalle and Howard, and Carnap quietly corrected it (Logical Syntax §50) — the Circle's trophy was partly its own misreading (Uebel §3.7); (3) operationalism in Bridgman's form makes a concept as many concepts as there are operations — the departure that Putnam's gold/Archimedes case turns into an argument for realism.

## Explanation plan

1. **The problem.** The train: lightning at both ends; the embankment observer says simultaneous, the passenger says the front stroke came first; relativity says neither is wrong. Sub-problem: how can two observers disagree about *when* without one being mistaken? Obvious attempt: there is a fact of the matter and one observer has it; the other's clocks are slow. Why it fails: the 1905 paper denies that 'simultaneous at a distance' has any meaning until a procedure is given, and once the procedure is given the two verdicts are both correct outputs of it in different frames.
2. **The artefact, read closely.** §1: a clock at A fixes 'A-time' for events at A; for distant events one needs a rule; the rule — light out and back, equal times each way — *defines* synchrony. Einstein's own words: 'by definition'. Then the consequence, derived in two lines for this reader: observers in relative motion applying the same rule get different simultaneity classes (the Putnam boy-scouts example makes it concrete: two pistols fired, each observer 'right' that his fired first).
3. **What the Circle saw.** Mach's precept — a concept with no procedure is idle — vindicated by the most celebrated result in physics; the a priori form of space and time emptied by physics; Schlick and Reichenbach: the synchrony rule is a *coordinative definition* — a stipulation linking a theoretical term to a physical procedure, like choosing a unit of length — hence conventional, hence the geometry of spacetime is partly convention plus empirical content (the relative a priori). Ayer in 1978 still names it as the vindication.
4. **Where the reading departs from the physics — first departure.** Einstein's definition is a stipulation *inside* a theory, not a reduction *to* observation: it uses the postulate of light's constant speed (empirical) and the symmetry of the out-and-back path. The verificationist reading says 'simultaneous' *means* the procedure; the physics says the procedure is the *only one definable from the theory's own causal structure* — Malament's result (stated, not proved): standard synchrony is uniquely definable from the light-cone relation. So the 'free convention' is not free; a different $\varepsilon$ is not another meaning but a non-invariant coordinate choice. The criterion's trophy turns out to be a theorem about structure, which is Carnap's 'framework' reading rather than Schlick's 'verification' one.
5. **Second departure.** The Schlick–Reichenbach coordinative-definition reading of *general* relativity is faulted by later historians (Ryckman, DiSalle, Howard — Uebel §3.7): the metric is not a convention coordinated to rods and clocks but is determined by the field equations; Carnap's Logical Syntax §50 already corrected the reading without saying so. State this as the 'what refuted it' layer: the Circle's best example was read through its own doctrine.
6. **Third departure, forward.** Operationalism in Bridgman's form: a concept is its operation; so 'length by tape' and 'length by light' are two concepts. Putnam's gold: Archimedes' operation and the periodic table's operation; a piece that passes the first and fails the second was never gold, and it 'would have behaved differently' under tests Archimedes could not run. The operation is evidence for the kind, not the kind — the Kripke–Putnam module's thesis, previewed in one paragraph.
7. **Verdict.** Which module results the reading used (criterion; geometry as definitions; frameworks and the relative a priori); where the artefact departs (the three points); what survived — the demand for a procedure as a *test of a concept's content* (Einstein's own method), separated from the claim that the procedure is the meaning.

## EXERCISE PLAN

1. (warm-up) From the synchrony rule, derive that two events simultaneous for the embankment observer are not simultaneous for the passenger, giving the sign of the difference in terms of the train's direction.
2. (refute) "Relativity proves that the verification principle is correct: a concept without a procedure is meaningless." Break it: state what §1 actually asserts (a definition inside a theory using an empirical postulate), and give the sentence in the theory — the constancy of light — that is not itself defined by any single procedure.
3. (derivation) Reichenbach's $\varepsilon$: write the general synchrony rule $t_B = t_A + \varepsilon\,(t'_A - t_A)$ and show that only $\varepsilon = \tfrac12$ makes synchrony symmetric and transitive within a frame; then state what Malament's result adds (uniqueness from the causal structure alone).
4. (boundary probe) Take two events on a single worldline (timelike-separated). Show that the synchrony rule is not needed to order them and that every observer agrees; then say what this does to the claim that 'simultaneity is conventional' — conventional for which pairs?
5. (claim–evidence) Ayer in 1978 cites quantum position/velocity beside simultaneity as a vindication. Using the lesson's three readings, say which reading the uncertainty relation supports and which it does not; name the fact about the theory that decides it.
6. (FORWARD → necessary-a-posteriori) Archimedes' operation for gold and the periodic table's operation disagree on a sample. On the operationalist reading there are two concepts; on the realist reading one kind. Write the counterfactual the realist needs ('the sample would have behaved differently under…') and say what it presupposes about the kind. The solution says the Kripke module calls the presupposition 'the kind has an essence found by science', with a `<Ref>`.
7. (research-shaped) Perrin's determination of Avogadro's number by several independent methods (Brownian motion, radioactivity, blackbody radiation) agreeing to within a few percent is the standard case of theory-mediated measurement. State what the agreement establishes that no single operation could, and why the operationalist cannot explain the *agreement*.

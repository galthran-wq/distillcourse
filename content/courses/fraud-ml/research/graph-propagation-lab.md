# Lab: fraud propagation and a GCN on a transaction graph — research brief

Lesson: `graph-propagation-lab` (module: graphs, type: lab, planned). NEW.
Goal: Build a graph fraud scorer — sparse adjacency, connected components, a fraud-personalised random walk with restart with time-decayed weights, egonet features and a two-layer GCN in numpy — and score an Elliptic subset.

## ARC
- Inherits: representations, ER edges and time decay (fraud-as-a-graph); union-find, A^k, egonet features (graph-features-and-communities); RWR, power iteration, the 20-node toy's ranking (guilt-by-association); Â, the GCN layer, Skip-GCN, the temporal split (graph-neural-networks); the Elliptic reading (elliptic-illicit-nodes); the numpy backprop pattern (anomaly-scorers-lab).

- Spine step 6 (final item): "…recovered by propagating known fraud through the graph as a random walk with restart, or by learning the neighbourhood with a graph network."

# Lab: the pipeline on the simulator stream — research brief

Lesson: `pipeline-capstone-lab` (module: system, type: lab, planned). NEW. The course's capstone lab.
Goal: Assemble the full detector on the handbook simulator's stream — point-in-time feature lookup, daily scoring with a top-k alert queue, feedbacks and delayed labels feeding a retraining cadence, a stability monitor, the PSD2 fraud-rate controller and an alert ledger — and report cumulative card precision over the stream.

## LAB PLAN

Artifact: one notebook producing `fraudsys.py`-style tooling — `FeatureLog.asof`, `daily_alert_queue`, `label_arrival`, `retrain_feedback_delayed`, `stability_index`, `FraudRateController`, `AlertLedger` — driven by a shipped `run_stream(days)` loop over 90 simulated days, ending with a cumulative card-precision score on a held-out stream and a ledger/report the learner can print.

CANON GROUNDING: modelled on FraudFinder notebooks 02, 04 and 07 (dossier: "batch window features … Feature Store ingestion with feature_time"; "point-in-time batch serving to a training table … online prediction assembling features from Feature Store"; "Cloud Run app triggered by Pub/Sub … online feature lookup, endpoint predict, request/response logs") — the series' end-to-end assembly that the canon has learners run — and on Dal Pozzolo 2018's Algorithm 1 (the daily loop with feedbacks and delayed samples, k = 100 cards, δ = 7) which the module-8 lesson derived; the York EECS 4486 project ("apply a technology to a reporting/monitoring process") and the USI case presentations are the course-level precedent for an integrative assignment. Departures: cloud services become pandas/numpy over a simulated stream; the series' omitted controls (delay block, budget, feedback classifier, parity check, monitoring with lag) are the lab's checkpoints; the PSD2 controller and the AMLR record have no canon assignment and are added because modules 9–10 specified them as components — stated as the lab's extension beyond the canon; no graph layer or rule layer is built here (graph-propagation-lab built the former; rules are shipped as a fixed blocking rule).

Dataset: the reduced simulator generator (shipped complete; 120 days: days 1–30 as history, 31–90 as the learner's stream, 91–120 as the held-out stream for the metric), with label-arrival timestamps (dispute delay ~ exponential, mean 20 days, 15% never disputed, investigator feedback same day for alerted cards) and the module-3 feature module imported complete; the held-out stream's labels and card ids in `private/`. Licence: our own generator.

Checkpoints (plan; lab-author may split or deepen):
1. `feature-asof-lookup` (exact, warm-up): `FeatureLog.asof(entity_ids, t)` returning the last value ≤ t per entity from a (entity, feature_time, values) log; verified on a seeded log; wrong variants: ≤ replaced by <; latest-value read used for a past t.
2. `daily-alert-queue` (exact): score day t's rows with the current model, collapse to cards (max score), take the top k = 100 cards, emit the feedback rows (all of the day's transactions of alerted cards); verified on a seeded day; consumes 1; wrong variants: transaction-level top-k; feedback rows limited to the alerted transaction only.
3. `label-arrival` (exact): the label state at day t — feedbacks available for alerted cards at once, delayed labels for day t − δ rows whose dispute has arrived, silence read as genuine at t − δ, cards blocked since removed from the scoreable set; verified on a seeded 20-day run against a hand-built label table; consumes 2; wrong variants: delayed labels read at t instead of t − δ; blocked cards kept.
4. `feedback-delayed-retrain` (exact): nightly retraining of two models — F on the last Q days of feedbacks, D on the last M days of delayed samples — averaged at scoring time, versus one pooled model; the Forget policy (all past frauds + a genuine window) as the D window; verified by score digests on a seeded day after a fixed number of days (library tree model allowed; the loop is the content); consumes 1, 2, 3; wrong variants: pooled only; D trained on rows after t − δ.
5. `stability-monitor` (exact): PSI of the score distribution per 7-day period against the training baseline (ten quantile bins fixed on the baseline), traffic lights at 0.10/0.25; verified on seeded distributions incl. a planted drift; wrong variants: bins re-fitted each period; natural log vs log₂ mismatch with the thresholds.
6. `fraud-rate-controller` (exact): the rolling 90-day value-weighted fraud rate per band (≤ 100 / 250 / 500 by amount) with recovered funds counted and SCA'd (non-exempted) flows in the denominator, and the state machine (ok → reported → suspended after two consecutive quarters above → resumable after one quarter at or below); verified on a seeded quarterly series; wrong variants: count-weighted rate; exempted-only denominator; trip after one quarter.
7. `alert-ledger` (exact): a per-alert record (alert id, day, card, score, top-3 attributions from the model's feature contributions — a shipped helper computes them, band, action, disposition, feedback timestamp, dispute timestamp, escalated flag) appended for every alert whether or not escalated, queryable by day and card; verified by a digest of the ledger after a seeded run; consumes 2, 3; wrong variants: ledger rows only for escalated alerts; disposition overwritten on dispute instead of appended.
8. `parity-report` (review, optional): the learner replays one day through the serving path (per-row as-of reads) and through the batch feature module, diffs the features, and names the feature that disagrees and why (the shipped batch module includes the current row in one window — the module-3 leak reinstated deliberately); rubric requires the feature name and the mechanism; "they differ" alone insufficient.
9. `cumulative-card-precision` (metric, optional, LAST): cumulative CP@100 over the held-out stream (days 91–120) from the learner's assembled system (any model, any strategy; the server recomputes from submitted daily alert sets and the private labels with detected-card removal); threshold calibrated so the reference system (tuned tree, F/D averaged, Forget) clears by ≥ 0.02 and a static model trained once on days 1–30 does not (reference on our reduced simulator ≈ 0.27 vs static ≈ 0.21 — to calibrate).

Final open task: checkpoint 9 — metric cumulative CP@100; rough threshold ≈ 0.24 (calibrate).

Known-wrong variants: as-of with <; transaction-level queue; delayed labels at t; blocked cards kept; pooled-only retraining; D trained past t − δ; re-fitted PSI bins; count-weighted rate; one-quarter trip; escalated-only ledger; overwritten dispositions.

NOT blanked (ship complete): the generator with label-arrival timestamps, the feature module (module 3), the harness (module 2), the model fits (library), the attribution helper, the fixed blocking rule, `run_stream` (the driver that calls the learner's components in order and plots), plotting (daily CP@100 and cumulative; PSI per period with lights; the controller's quarterly rate vs reference with state badges; ledger summary table).

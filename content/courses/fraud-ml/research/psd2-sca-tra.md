## Key points
1. The actions the law names: SCA (two independent elements, dynamically linked to amount and payee for remote payments) is the step-up of score-to-action; the exemptions are "approve without friction" — and TRA is the one exemption that depends on a model.
2. TRA derived as a constraint: three conditions — (a) the PSP's rate for the type ≤ the band's reference, (b) amount ≤ ETV, (c) six real-time flags absent; Art 18(3)'s "risk scoring for each individual transaction" as the regulatory definition of the module's detector; Art 2(2)'s minimum monitoring factors as a feature checklist (compromised-element lists, amount, known scenarios, malware, device logs); the Annex table read: the higher the band, the lower the tolerated rate (13× for cards between EUR 100 and 500).
3. The fraud rate precisely (Art 19): value-weighted, 90-day rolling, numerator counts fraud whether or not recovered, denominator includes SCA'd and exempted flows — three consequences derived: a few large frauds move the rate; SCA'd volume dilutes it (so a PSP cannot game by moving traffic between lanes without changing the rate's meaning); exempted frauds are the PSP's own liability (Art 74(2)).
4. The controller (Art 20): report immediately on breach; trip after two consecutive quarters above; reset after one quarter at or below; per type and per band; the hysteresis drawn; the yearly audit of the model and methodology (Art 3(2), 19(2)–(3)); the quarterly report's fields (Art 21) — the capstone's fraud-rate-controller and reporting checkpoints.
5. The liability shift (Arts 72–74): refund by the next business day unless reasonable fraud suspicion in writing; EUR 50 payer cap; no payer loss without SCA; recorded use is not proof — so a false negative under an exemption is the issuer's loss, a false positive is friction and a lost sale: the cost matrix of module 2 with its legal column filled; why issuers invest in scoring.
6. The policy computed: exempt iff score < τ_band within each band, subject to the six flags; the trade between exempted volume (friction saved) and the rate; the τ at which the EUR 250 band is lawful; the band at which no τ works for a given model's precision — "model precision sets the legally allowed friction" derived as an inequality: with π_r the remote fraud rate by value and precision p(τ) of the exempted set's complement, the exempted set's fraud rate is a function of τ that must stay under the reference.
7. ★ Starred: the other exemptions as a table (Arts 10–17 with their numbers); dynamic linking's batch/blocked-amount cases; traceability (Art 29) as the audit log; MOTO's exclusion as a known channel risk; EBA's review and the consolidated-text caveat.

## Set pieces & gems
- The Annex table: 0.13% / 0.06% / 0.01% — the only published regulator-set precision-type target.
- Art 18(3)'s "risk scoring for each individual transaction".
- Art 19's "whether the funds have been recovered or not".
- Art 20's two-quarter trip and one-quarter reset.
- Art 74(2): no payer loss without SCA.
- Art 72(2): recorded use "shall in itself not necessarily be sufficient".
- Rec 95: MOTO needs no equivalent protection.
- EUR 30 / EUR 100 / five (Art 16) vs TRA (Art 18): the numbering correction the dossier flags.

## EXERCISE PLAN
1. Warm-up: a PSP processed EUR 48m of remote card payments in 90 days with EUR 31,200 of fraud (EUR 4,000 recovered); compute the Art 19 rate and state which bands' TRA it may use.
2. Derivation the lesson skipped: write the exempted set's fraud rate as a function of τ given the score's precision–recall curve on remote transactions and the value distribution; show the rate is monotone in τ under a monotone score and derive the condition on the model's value-weighted precision for the EUR 500 band to be reachable at all.
3. Boundary probe: a PSP that applies SCA to everything — its rate's denominator still includes all remote flows; what the rate measures then and why the exemption's lawfulness is judged on a population the exemption did not select; and a PSP whose exempted flow is 100% of remote payments — the rate is the exempted set's rate.
4. Claim to refute: "Moving all high-amount transactions to SCA lowers the fraud rate and therefore permits exempting more low-amount ones." Refute: SCA'd flows stay in the denominator; the rate falls only if their fraud falls; and the per-band rule is checked per band; name the clause (Art 19(1)) that defeats it.
5. Research-shaped: on the simulator (treating all transactions as remote card payments), compute the 90-day value-weighted fraud rate per band under "exempt if score < τ" for a grid of τ with the module-2 harness's model; report the largest lawful τ per band.
6. FORWARD (E7): take the lawful τ for the EUR 250 band and compute the savings (example-dependent-cost) of the exemption policy against "SCA everything", counting friction as a lost-sale cost per step-up — the reader has built the engine's objective → lands on `tra-exemption-engine`.
7. Mixed review (E3): the controller is a drift detector with legal thresholds (concept-drift, model-risk-monitoring): compute its detection delay (two quarters) against a Page–Hinkley detector on the weekly rate, and state why the law chose hysteresis over sensitivity.

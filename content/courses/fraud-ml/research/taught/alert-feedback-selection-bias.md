**Problem.** The freshest labels are the investigators' verdicts on the cards yesterday's model ranked highest: of what are they a sample, can the bias be weighted away, and how should they be combined with the complete delayed labels? Answer: alert feedback is complete selection bias (the label enters through the card; zero selection probability for a fraud kind the model scores low on cards with nothing else it scores high); importance weighting needs positivity and P(s=1|x,y), which for alerts is known only a delay late, undefined where the model never looks, and even exact shrinks the frauds to the stream's rarity (measured: weighted feedback model worse, though the weighted run also changed the forest; the €500 random-selection check pins the failure on the selection); keep the streams in two models and average their posteriors (α = ½), which on real data beats pooling at the queue.

- Proposition (with proof) "the posterior a selected sample teaches" (at x with P(−|x) > 0 and P(s=1|x,+), P(s=1|x,−) > 0): sample odds = stream odds × P(s=1|x,+)/P(s=1|x,−); factor 1 (covariate shift: posterior right wherever there are rows), 1/β (undersampling, divided out by the β correction), function of x (complete bias) — same ###

- Active learning defined (call the cards the model is least sure about) and rejected on the next-call value (Ref economics-of-detection the-value-of-the-next-call) — ### Choosing the calls to be informative instead

# change-detectors — ledger

NOTE (data): all detector numbers were computed by the writer (numba implementations; `river` 0.26.1 agrees on ADWIN delays and supplied the three-segment row). A detector restarts after each alarm (ADWIN's α = 0.2 MTFA and the DDM per-stream counts re-measured; DDM has no MTFA); ADWIN checks every 32 items at geometric split points. Horizon for a miss 60,000 items. Normal-noise table: 4M items, 4,000 runs. Weekly-rhythm rows re-measured on 40M days (C), 4,000–20,000 change runs. Two-window rows re-measured on 40M+ stable items per level, 4,000 change runs.

**Problem.** What rule (a *change detector*; "detector" = that rule, "the model" = the fraud model, fixed in the intro) on the running error says a rise is real, and what does it cost in delay and false alarms? Answer: score rules by MTFA, MTD(θ), MDR(θ) at equal MTFA; CUSUM accumulates evidence with a floor (a max over possible start points of a log likelihood ratio), Page–Hinkley replaces μ with the running mean, DDM uses binomial noise and is slower the longer the calm, reference/sliding windows test two stretches (handover or full restart after an alarm), ADWIN tests every split with a threshold charged for the splits. On payments feed the share of compromised cards missed (slow) and CP@k (fast), never raw error; the alarm rings at c + δ + MTD and replaces a schedule only when retrains are further apart than the MTD.

## Notation
- x_t stream value, μ, σ usual level and sd, z_t standardised, θ size of change in σ; Δ raw rise in the rate itself (DDM price section, exercises 2(c) and 3; in 3(b) a rise per step)
- CUSUM: g_t, S_t plain sum, allowance v, threshold h (NOT δ, λ, k: those stay label delay, EWMA rate, alert budget). Libraries call them delta and threshold/lambda
- PH: x̄_t running mean, m_T, M_T, PH_T; same v, h in x's units
- DDM: p_t, s_t, p_min, s_min; T length of the calm, n items after the change, Δ the raw rise, σ = √(p(1−p)) there
- Windows: n_0, n_1, μ̂_0, μ̂_1; ADWIN: W, W_0, W_1, m, ε_cut, α (not δ), α' = α/ln|W|, L = ln(4|W|/α) in exercises
- c day of the change, δ label delay (from concept-drift); TP, γ and the 1,000 alerts in the six-world table count payments over a stretch, each fraud standing for one compromised card; counted per card per day they become CP@k's numerator, γ_t and k of alert-budget-metrics

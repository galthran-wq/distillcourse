# imbalance-techniques-revisited — ledger

**Problem.** The rare-class toolkit (class weights, oversampling, SMOTE,
undersampling, balanced ensembles) was judged by ROC-AUC and balanced
accuracy; under AP and CP@100 on forward folds, which help, and why does a
remedy raise one metric and lower another? Answer: every remedy moves the
boundary into the genuine cloud; balanced accuracy rewards any cut moved
toward the base rate; a remedy can buy ROC area at FPRs the budget never
reaches while blurring the top into mixed tied blocks; on simulated and real
card data the best setting for AP is no or the mildest rebalancing, for AUC
near balance, and CP@100 is unmoved beyond noise on the simulator but loses
3–11 cards/day on real data; the ratio is a hyperparameter chosen by CP@k on
prequential folds with the sampler inside each fold; balanced ensembles gain
most at the mildest ratios, so not through balance (tree diversity is the
explanation that fits, not demonstrated); XGBoost without rebalancing has the
best or near-best AP on both streams, but the real-data CP@100 pick is a
mild weight (ω = 5) or a balanced ensemble, ahead of plain XGBoost only at
noise level.

## Explained
- Definition: imbalance ratio IR = frauds/genuine (small: ≈ 0.009 on the simulator stretch holding every prequential fold and test week (Ref canonical-datasets calendar §); Belgian stream ≈ 0.0025); exact slice counts only in Exercise 1; target ratio r of a remedy (r = 1 balanced); undersampling to r keeps β = IR/r (Ref undersampling-calibration) — intro
- Toy: make_classification 5,000 points, 5% minority, class_sep 0.5; depth-5 tree; one fold of 1,000 test points, 54 frauds — §What every remedy does / The remedies, side by side on one toy
- Class weight ω = fraud weight / genuine weight; "balanced" ω = 1/IR (toy 19); random undersampling to 216/216 of 3,784 genuine — same §
- Step-through (surface-1..3, key image): flagged at 0.5 / frauds: 68/36 → 106/48 (weighted) → 139/52 (undersampled); Task: 71 more flagged, 16 more fraud — same §
- Weights, oversampling copies (weight = copies in expectation) and undersampling (weight β on genuine, ω = 1/β) are one move: missed fraud dearer than false alarm, learner labels more of the overlap fraud; leaf 1 fraud : 5 genuine relabelled at ω = 19 (KeyFact) — §Why every remedy moves the boundary the same way
- SMOTE defined: new = x + u(x_nb − x), x_nb one of 5 nearest fraud neighbours, u ~ U[0,1]; worked (30, 1,000)→(62, 3,200), u = 0.75 → (54, 2,650); count: r = 0.5 on 3,784 genuine → 1,892 frauds → 1,676 synthetic (~8 per real fraud); ignores genuine payments, segments cross genuine regions in overlapping tasks; in-body exercise u = 0.25 → (31.5, 1,175), SMOTE 0.5 + RUS 1 keeps β = 0.5 — §Synthetic frauds: SMOTE
- Balanced accuracy BA = ½(TP/n₁ + TN/n₀); adding a payment with fraud probability q raises E[BA] iff q/(1−q) > n₁/n₀ = IR ⇒ BA's best cut flags every payment above the base rate = where 0.5 on a balanced set cuts (Ref undersampling-calibration threshold §); "the metric a balanced training set is built to win" — §Which metrics see the move / Balanced accuracy rewards any cut…
- Toy LR, five folds: ω = 19 lifts BA 0.641 → 0.899, AUC 0.937 both, AP 0.535 → 0.536 (well-specified LR: weight adds ln ω to the intercept, order unchanged — recalled from classical-ml imbalance-leakage, not re-derived) — same §
- Tree weights change splits, hence the order; toy five-fold table (AUC/AP/BA): none 0.906/0.528/0.786; ω 19 0.887/0.471/0.898; ROS 0.880/0.456/0.888; SMOTE 0.913/0.499/0.910; RUS 0.913/0.408/0.896; SMOTE 0.5 + RUS 1 0.915/0.494/0.912; BA up for all, AP down for all, AUC either way within fold spread (KeyFact: the metrics read different parts of the ranking — BA one cut, AUC every threshold equally, AP mostly the top; only AP reads what a budgeted queue works, and on the toy it says no remedy helped) — §The ranking metrics: one number unmoved, one falling
- AUC-up/AP-down: opened as the same split the reader saw for the full-depth vs depth-2 tree (0.788/0.309 vs 0.763/0.496, Ref alert-budget-metrics no-information § and delayed-labels-prequential what-AUC-sees §), now produced by a remedy: on the step-through fold RUS AUC 0.90 → 0.94, AP 0.49 → 0.42; curves must cross (Ref dominance §); toy budget line = notional 50 alerts on the 1,000-point fold (~5%, FPR 50/946 ≈ 0.05), vs simulator ≤ 0.012; AUC gain from tail frauds lifted above ~90% of genuine, ≈ 0.9/54 ≈ 0.017 each; three such ≈ 0.05 > the 0.04 jump, the excess offset by area lost left of the crossing (low FPR); all gains at FPR ≥ 0.05 — §Why a remedy can raise ROC-AUC… / Where the undersampled tree wins ROC area
- Top block: the full-depth tree's tie mechanism at the other end of the list; RUS tree has 51 test points tied at score exactly 1, 20 fraud (precision 0.39, to recall 0.37) because the 216 kept genuine rows (1 in 17.5) left the lower-right region with no genuine rows; tie convention (Ref) counts all 20 at 0.39 (vs the full tree's 0-tied frauds at the base rate); every fold: 51–72 tied, precision 0.32–0.56 vs 2–7 for the plain tree. ## Notation
- IR imbalance ratio (frauds/genuine); r target ratio of a remedy; ω class-weight ratio (fraud weight / genuine weight), = scale_pos_weight; β = IR/r (keep rate, from undersampling-calibration)
- BA balanced accuracy; n₁, n₀ numbers of frauds and genuine payments; q probability that an added flagged payment is fraud
- SMOTE: x, x_nb, u, x_new

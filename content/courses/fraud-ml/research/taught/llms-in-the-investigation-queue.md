**Problem.** An alert reaches an investigator: may a language model decide it, explain it, or draft the report, what checks its output, and what stays with a person? Answer: not as a scorer (base-rate precision alone; a slow nearest-neighbour tally with retrieval; seconds vs milliseconds); after detection as a drafter (triage record with cited evidence, narrative from top-ten attributions, report draft) behind a grounding check and counterfactual tests, routed to the referral band only; the call, risk profile, onboarding, report and signature stay with people (AMLR 18(3), 76(5); AMLA draft paras 78–79, 87–91).

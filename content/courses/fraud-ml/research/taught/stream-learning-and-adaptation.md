**Problem.** How should a learner on a stream remember, forget and replace itself, and how to score it while both change? Answer: predict, then score, then learn; report with a sliding or fading window; Kappa at the model's own flagging rate, or for a budgeted model a whole-week window of CP@k. A memory rule is a bet about how relevance falls with age. Blind forgetting trades recovery against calm accuracy, a detector gets both when the change is large; replacement can be local. A weighted pool hedges; bagging does not forget unless a detector makes it. Fraud: chunks and nightly retraining; choose memory by what each bet can lose: about 90 days (at worst 0.09 short of the best row; 30 days is 0.14 short when a method returns), or every fraud with a shorter genuine window, which matches it in calm and on a return at a third to a thirteenth of the genuine rows and lags 0.06–0.10 on a new method; cut back on an alarm you trust. One day is starved; no strategy wins every world. Keeping frauds shows no gain on a 90-day window (K_gen = 90, a separate pass, is within noise of K = 90 in every column).

# Research brief: antichrist-applied-genealogy (The Antichrist: Genealogy Run on Jesus and Paul)

status: planned
module: third-treatise
type: core (module application, L16)
goal: Analyze The Antichrist §§24–47 as an application of the three treatises' machinery to a historical record, naming which Genealogy results each step uses and where the history outruns the evidence.

## ARC (L10)

- **Inherits:** The three treatises reconstructed and joined: the priestly inversion (I), guilt as debt to God (II), the priest's redirection and the will to truth (III); the Doctrine of Types; the Four Great Errors (doer-behind-the-deed).
- **Contributes:** Nietzsche's own application of the machinery to one case — Israel's God denaturalized by priests (A §§24–26), Jesus reconstructed as a type (§§27–35), Paul's inversion of the practice into a doctrine (§§39–47) — with each step's Genealogy source named and its evidence graded: where the account is mechanism-plus-record and where it is assertion (the is–ought slides the Hollingdale reader flags; the Manu comparison; the Borgia counterfactual). The lesson shows the method working and shows its limits on Nietzsche's own case.
- **Hands off:** To Module 5 (soul-as-society-of-drives): the 'type' the Antichrist reconstructs Jesus as is a drive-constitution, and the psychology behind it is BGE's; to the audit.

Spine step: "Third Treatise: the ascetic ideal is what a declining life does with suffering — the priest redirects 'someone is to blame' onto the sufferer — and modern science is its latest form because it rests on an unconditional faith in truth; the critique reaches Nietzsche's own will to truth, and The Antichrist runs the machinery on Jesus and Paul."

**APPLICATION spec (L16).** Artefact: The Antichrist (autumn 1888), Nietzsche's own deployment of the Genealogy's method on a historical record he cross-references directly (A §24 and §45 cite GM). Module results used: I:7 and I:16 (the priestly inversion; §§24–26), II:19–21 (the debt to a god; §§25–26's 'moral world-order'), III:15 (the redirection; §§40–43's disciples and Paul), the Four Great Errors (§15's 'imaginary causes'), the Doctrine of Types (§§29–31's 'redeemer type'). Departure from the idealisation: the Genealogy's method demands a 'real history' (Preface §7); the Antichrist supplies a psychology of types and a reading of the Gospels but almost no documentary history — the five-stage denaturalizing of Israel's God has no dated sources, the 'idiot' type rests on Dostoevsky and Renan's negation, Paul's motives are asserted (§42 'genius of hatred'), and the value verdicts (§§2, 62) slide from 'proceeds from weakness' to 'bad' without the premise. The lesson records each as a grade, not as a dismissal.

## Key points the lesson must cover

1. **§§24–26: Israel's God denaturalized — GM I:7 and II:19–21 applied.** The five stages: a national god of strength and joy → misfortune → the priests' reinterpretation of history as reward and punishment → the 'moral world-order' → the priest as the sole channel to God; 'the priestly falsification'; the 'holy lie'; the Jews as 'the most fateful people' because they inverted (I:7's seed). Map each stage to its Genealogy source and grade: mechanism (I:7's priestly inversion; II:19–21's debt to a god) plus a reading of the Old Testament as a priestly redaction — a thesis with a 19th-century scholarly base (Wellhausen) Nietzsche does not cite; evidence: none dated in the text.
2. **§§27–35: the redeemer type — the Doctrine of Types applied.** Not hero, not genius (against Renan); 'idiot' in Dostoevsky's sense (the deleted word; Kaufmann); a physiological over-sensitivity to contact producing 'resist not evil' as instinct; the Kingdom of Heaven as an inner state; the Gospel as a practice ('a new way of living, not a new belief'); the death on the Cross as the demonstration, not a sacrifice. Reconstruct: (P) a type explains a doctrine; (P) the doctrine (non-resistance, no guilt, no distance) fits one type (hypersensitivity to pain); (C) the historical Jesus was that type and taught nothing else. Grade: a type-hypothesis with the Gospels read selectively as evidence; the method is the Genealogy's (type-facts explain values) with the record thinner.
3. **§§39–47: Paul — III:15 and I:16 applied.** 'There has been only one Christian, and he died on the Cross'; the disciples' ressentiment after the death; the invention of resurrection, judgment, immortality; Paul as 'genius of hatred', the 'dysangelist' who made the death a sacrifice and the doctrine a tool of priestly power; §45's verse-by-verse reading of the New Testament; Pilate's 'What is truth?' as the one respectable line. Map: the redirection (III:15) run on the disciples' 'someone must be to blame'; the priestly inversion (I:7, 16) run on Paul; the debt to God (II:21) run on the sacrificial reading of the Cross. Grade: the mechanism is the Genealogy's; the motives are asserted; the New Testament readings are checkable and some hold (the quoted verses are quoted).
4. **§15 and the Four Great Errors.** Christianity as a system of imaginary causes and effects (sin, redemption, grace, punishment) laid over physiological states — the cannon-shot dream at the scale of a religion. Map to Twilight; grade: the mechanism is general and the application is by fiat.
5. **The is–ought slides — the missing-premise exercise made explicit.** §2: 'What is good? All that heightens the feeling of power… What is bad? All that proceeds from weakness.' §7: pity 'thwarts the law of evolution'. §62: 'the one immortal blemish of mankind'. For each: the descriptive premise (a type-fact or a physiological claim), the value conclusion, and the unstated bridge (life-enhancement as the standard — BGE 4; will to power as the measure — Module 5). The lesson names the bridge and defers its defence to will-to-power-as-psychology and value-creation-and-normativity.
6. **§§56–57 and §61 as the method's limits.** Manu's caste code praised against the Bible ('a woman's mouth… pure' vs. 1 Corinthians 7:9) — a comparison of two 'holy lies' by their effect on the body; Cesare Borgia as Pope as the counterfactual victory — 'Christianity would have been abolished'. Grade: value-verdicts and counterfactuals with no mechanism; the method applied past its evidence. The composition facts (thirty days, the abandoned four-book plan, 'A Curse') as the frame, in two sentences.

## Pitfalls / subtleties to flag

- The Antichrist is the most inflammatory text in the corpus; the register stays dry, the hyperbole quoted, the verdicts graded. Never paraphrase §62 in the course's voice.
- Nietzsche's history of Israel and of the early church is not the course's history; the lesson evaluates it as an application of a method and does not supply the modern scholarship (a one-line pointer to Wellhausen as Nietzsche's likely source suffices).
- The 'idiot' word is a philological case, not a diagnosis; do not let it become one.
- The is–ought slides are the reader's most important exercise in this module; give them a section, not a footnote.
- Do not let the lesson expand into Buddhism (§§20–23) beyond one sentence; it is not needed for the application.

## EXERCISE PLAN (floor: ≤1 warm-up; ≥2 of boundary / refute / derivation / research; one FORWARD)

1. (warm-up) For §§25–26, §§29–31 and §42, name the Genealogy section whose mechanism is being applied.
2. (missing premise) Take §2's four definitions and §7's verdict on pity; for each, write the descriptive premise, the value conclusion, and the bridge premise the text does not state; then find the one published sentence (BGE 4 or A §2's own 'feeling of power') that comes closest to stating the bridge, and say whether it is an argument.
3. (refute) "The Antichrist proves that Christianity was invented by Paul." Break it: distinguish the mechanism (III:15's redirection, I:7's inversion — argued in GM) from the historical claim (asserted in §42 with the New Testament as its only cited record); state what §45's verse readings do establish and what §42's 'genius of hatred' does not.
4. (derivation) Reconstruct §§29–33's redeemer type as an explicit argument from the Doctrine of Types (type-fact → doctrine), then derive what evidence would confirm or disconfirm a type-hypothesis about a historical person from texts written by others — and state whether the Gospels, on Nietzsche's own §§44–45 reading of them as falsified, can be that evidence.
5. (sourcing, corroboration) Read §56's Manu quotations against 1 Corinthians 7:9 as two sources: run records / argues / omits on Nietzsche's use of each (what a law code records, what he argues from it, what he omits — Manu's Chandala provisions, which Twilight 'Improvers' §3 itself quotes); state what the comparison can establish about 'holy lies' and what it cannot about their value.
6. (boundary probe) §61's Borgia counterfactual: 'Christianity would have been abolished'. State what the Genealogy's method (a real history) can say about a counterfactual (nothing), and what §61 is doing instead (a value-verdict staged as history); then say whether any part of it survives as an argument.
7. (FORWARD → soul-as-society-of-drives) The 'type' of §§29–31 is a constitution of drives with one dominant sensitivity. Write what a psychology would have to say for 'a type explains a doctrine' to be a causal claim (drives as the units; a rank-order among them), and find the sentence in BGE 12 that reopens the soul as such an order; the solution says Module 5 begins there.

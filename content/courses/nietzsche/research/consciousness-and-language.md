# Research brief: consciousness-and-language (Consciousness and language as herd instruments)

status: planned
module: the-human-being (2 of 9), lesson 7 of 45
type: core
goal: Explain Nietzsche's argument that consciousness developed for communication and therefore shows us only the common, average surface of ourselves

## ARC (L10)

- **Spine step (4, "The drive-self"):** "Before asking where values came from, Nietzsche asks who values: not a unified soul that sees itself clearly, but a society of drives with a thin conscious surface made for talking to the herd, and grammar's invented doer whom morality holds responsible."
- **Inherits:** from self-as-society-of-drives: the person as an ordered plurality of drives, most of whose work goes on below awareness (GS 333); the open question why consciousness feels like the whole self.
- **Contributes:** consciousness as a late, thin, social organ: it arose under the need to communicate, so it renders inner life in shared, average signs. Introduces `consciousness-as-communication`, `herd-instinct` (GS 116; used again in herd-morality-in-modern-dress, danger-of-dangers) and the unreliability of introspection (lessons 8, 11, 12).
- **Hands off:** to doer-behind-the-deed: if consciousness speaks the herd's language, its grammar (a subject for every verb) shapes what we take ourselves to be, and Nietzsche argues that it invents a doer.

## STANCE (binding)

- **Favorable:** Nietzsche argued from the structure of language, not from clinical data, that awareness is a late, partial layer shaped for transmission and that self-report averages the individual away; the unconscious of psychoanalysis and the confabulation findings of later psychology (lesson 12, off-path) came decades after GS 354.
- **Hard passages this lesson must quote whole:** BGE 268 (bge-zimmern.txt line 5502ff; communicability favours the ordinary, the rare "seldom propagate", the "progressus in simile"); GS 354 (pp.297-300; "herd nature", "the genius of the species", consciousness as "corruption, falsification, reduction to superficialities, and generalization"); GM II 16 (p.84; consciousness "their weakest and most fallible organ"). Presented as the argument's own conclusions about rank among human beings.
- **What the words meant:** Herde (herd) is the biological group as a unit of survival; GS 354 makes consciousness belong to "man's social or herd nature" and BGE 268 draws the consequence. "Average" (durchschnittlich) and "common" (gemein) measure frequency, and gemein also means ignoble; BGE 268 uses both senses. "Perspectivism" and "phenomenalism" in GS 354 name the result that the conscious world is "a surface- and sign-world", not a doctrine of relativity (perspectivism is lesson 29's).
- **Objection answered from his side:** the story is an untestable evolutionary conjecture, and animals without language appear conscious. Reply, texts first: the load-bearing part is the structure of communication, observable now: a word works only if it means the same across speakers, so what enters words is what speakers share (BGE 268; GS 354; D 115, pp.71-72); knowing is reducing the strange to the familiar, and the inner world is the most familiar and so least known (GS 355, pp.300-302; GS 335). The narrow reading saves the claim: GS 354 concerns reflective consciousness, "seeing itself in a mirror"; GS 357 (p.305) credits Leibniz with consciousness as "only one state" of representation. The gap, in one sentence: Nietzsche stipulates rather than argues that non-linguistic awareness is not what "consciousness" names, and the history in GS 354 is offered without evidence.
- **Humanizing reading to test:** the reading (Kaufmann, Ch. 9 I) that Nietzsche never denies the efficacy of consciousness, only flesh/spirit dualism. These texts call consciousness "a surface" (EH II 9, p.254) and unnecessary for most of life (GS 354); TI "Errors" 3 (p.495), "the will no longer moves anything", goes further and is lesson 11's. The lesson keeps to GS 354's claim and names the fork below.
- **Facts against the popular image:** Nietzsche is read as an irrationalist; GS 11 (pp.84-85) sets the task as incorporating knowledge into instinct, and GS 354 calls consciousness unfinished, not worthless.

## The reconstruction

- **Claim and where.** GS 354: consciousness developed only under the need to communicate; hence what becomes conscious is the communicable, the average. BGE 268 draws the social consequence; D 119 the epistemic one.
- **Argument in plain steps.** (1) Thinking, feeling, willing and remembering can occur without entering consciousness, and most of life does (GS 354; GS 333). (2) So consciousness needs a special explanation. (3) The most endangered animal needed help and had to say what it needed (GS 354). (4) Saying requires signs shared by the group (BGE 268). (5) Consciousness grew with that capacity: a thought becomes conscious as it takes the form of words. (6) So consciousness renders inner life in the shared vocabulary, which captures what is common and misses what is individual (D 115). (7) So introspection misdescribes us as more average, simpler and more unified than we are (D 119; GS 335; BGE 268).
- **Unstated premise.** Step 5: that consciousness and linguistic articulation rise together; GS 354 asserts the identity, it does not argue it. Step 3 is a conjecture.
- **Strongest objection and Nietzsche's reply.** As in STANCE.
- **Connections.** self-as-society-of-drives; doer-behind-the-deed (GS 127; BGE 17); four-great-errors (TI "Errors" 4-5; GS 355); herd-morality-in-modern-dress, danger-of-dangers (GS 116); perspectivism; becoming-who-you-are (GS 335, EH II 9); on-truth-and-lies.

## Where the claim forks (named by content)

Whether consciousness does anything: a late accompaniment that registers and transmits but does not cause (TI "Errors" 3), or one organ among others, weak and fallible (GM II 16) but not idle (GS 354 says unnecessary for most of life, not all; GS 11's incorporation is a causal role). The lesson states both and keeps its claim to GS 354's; efficacy is decided, as far as Nietzsche decides it, in lessons 8 and 11.

- GS 354 does not say consciousness is useless; the epiphenomenal claim is TI "Errors" 3, lesson 11's.
- Step 3 stays a hypothesis; steps 4-6 carry the weight.
- Define "perspectivism" at GS 354 as the surface-and-sign-world result; do not pre-teach GM III 12.
- Herde defined once; BGE 268's rank consequence quoted whole, no distancing.
- OTL is one paragraph of foreshadowing; the essay is lesson 32's.

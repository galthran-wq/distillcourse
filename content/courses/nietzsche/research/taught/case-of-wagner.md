# Taught: case-of-wagner

## Problem
How can the same music be "tragedy reborn" in 1871 and "a disease" in 1888, and is the verdict more than taste or grudge? Answer: the pleasure did not change (EH II 6 on *Tristan*); the question did. From GS 370 Nietzsche asks of every work "is it hunger or superabundance that has here become creative?", reading backward from what a work supplies to the need it serves. Run through CW in print order, the test finds the module's diagnosis (shadow of God, decadence, passive nihilism, life-negating values) in one artist. Against the "optics" objection the charge of falsity (a work inconsistent with itself) survives; the charge of decadence rests on the ascending/declining standard, which the pamphlet asserts and does not argue.

## Explained
- The 1871 hope: BT Foreword quoted ("art is the highest task and the properly metaphysical activity of this life"), BT 19 quoted (German music "from Beethoven to Wagner", "a re-birth of tragedy"); music = the Dionysian will speaking, music drama = tragedy back — ### The hope of 1871 (figures friendship-timeline.svg, wagner-1871-portrait.jpg)
- Friendship dates: met Leipzig 1868; Tribschen (house on Lake Lucerne) 1869–72, EH II 5 "should not want to give away … at any price the days of Tribschen"; Bayreuth festival 1876; *Parsifal* text and HH cross 1878 (EH on HH 5, "two swords"); *Parsifal* premiere 1882; Wagner dies Venice Feb 1883 — same subsection
- L13 refutation, three explanations that fail (intro lists the same three: taste, the *Parsifal* legend, Bayreuth; no "quarrel") — ### Three explanations that fail. Stated plainly: every refuting text is N's own 1888 account (EH = his autobiography; NcW glossed as a selection of his earlier Wagner passages), which cannot settle the biographical cause, only the criterion he applied in 1888: (1) taste: EH II 6 quoted (*Tristan* unequalled, "great benefactor of my life"); (2) Parsifal legend (pagan broke because Wagner turned Christian), ruled out "as his reason" by his own dating: NcW "By the summer of 1876 … I said farewell to Wagner in my heart", Parsifal text 1878 = "the proof"; (3) Bayreuth: EH on HH 2 ("translated into German … German beer"), EH II 5 "reichsdeutsch" (glossed: patriot of the 1871 Empire), NcW "even to anti-Semitism"; but EH on HH 3 quoted: "not a break with Wagner: … a total aberration of my instincts", "narcotic art—for example, Wagnerian art", unpacked: the exhausted listener was N himself (Basel professorship without vocation), using the music to deaden a wrong life. Replacement: the music and its hold unchanged, what it was evidence of changed; GS 370 first paragraph quoted, cut before "romanticism" ("what I failed to recognize … their distinctive character"); KeyFact: a verdict about use does not depend on the critic's enjoyment, so it can condemn a work he still loves and can be checked without sharing his taste (figure bayreuth-festspielhaus.jpg)
- GS 370 test (concept romantic-pessimism): two kinds of sufferers (over-fullness → Dionysian art, can afford "the terrible deed"; impoverishment → rest, redemption from themselves, intoxication, anaesthesia, "a god for the sick, a healer and savior", logic, "warm narrowness") — ## Hunger or superabundance / ### Two kinds of suffering
- Backward inference ("from the work to the maker … from the ideal to those who need it … to the commanding need behind it") explained with the medicine-cabinet case (objects made for needs; need read off the object); obvious sort by desire (permanence vs change) fails, each desire ambiguous: destruction (overflow "pregnant with future" vs hatred: "consider our anarchists"), immortalizing (gratitude: Rubens (glossed: Flemish 17th-c. painter), Hafiz, Goethe vs "tyrannic will" branding things with "the image of his torture") — ### Reading a work backwards (figure hunger-overflow.svg; Task: place BT's Greek tragedy on it)
- Romantic pessimism (Remark; first named here, as the "distinctive character" of the 1887 look-back): art/philosophy from hunger; Schopenhauer and Wagner; opposite = Dionysian pessimism (superabundance). "Dionysian" changed sides: BT = intoxicated dissolution, Wagner its voice; GS 370 = strength that affords the terrible, intoxication now on the hunger list. (The boundary "ill maker can make superabundant art" is left to Ex 5 and CW 5's "healthy enough", not stated in the body.) — same section
- CW metadata: *A Musician's Problem*, "A Letter from Turin, May 1888", Leipzig autumn 1888, for German readers; motto *ridendo dicere severum*; Preface, 12 sections, Postscript, Second Postscript, Epilogue — ## The pamphlet read in order (figure pamphlet-map.svg = key image)
- Preface (German + Common): "I am just as much a child of my age as Wagner—i.e., I am a decadent … I recognised the fact"; decadence his "greatest preoccupation", "good and evil" a subdivision, "Morality denies life"; Wagner = "modernity in concentrated form"; "Wagner belongs only to my diseases" — reflexive diagnosis — ### Preface: the diagnostician is a patient
- CW 1–2: *Carmen* (Bizet 1875, glossed) as control case; "everything divine runs with light feet"; "assumes the listener is intelligent"; "Bizet makes me productive … no other touchstone"; love as "mortal hatred between the sexes" — ### Sections 1–2
- CW 3 (German + English): "his opera is the opera of salvation. Someone always wants to be saved"; *Erlösung* = redemption; the seven-case catalogue (figure redemption-catalogue.svg); Dutchman/"eternal Jew"/women passage quoted (abridged) and glossed (Wandering Jew legend; Dutchman a sea version; artist = wanderer, salvation = domestication; "woman" = redeemer in the plots + audience; "refined form of parasitism" meant as stated); Goethe joke in a clause only; "Holiness—the only remaining higher value still seen by the mob or by woman"; bearing: GS 370's "god for the sick", the shadow of God (redemption without the God), *Lohengrin*'s ban on questioning ("Thou must and shalt believe") vs truthfulness — ### Section 3
- CW 4: *Ring* plot paragraph (Wotan's contracts on his spear, order paid with stolen gold; grandson Siegfried breaks the spear, wins Brünnhilde; Siegfried murdered, Brünnhilde into the pyre, the gods burn); separate paragraph: Wagner wrote the text 1848–52 as a revolutionary, took part in an 1849 German uprising, fled into exile under warrant; read Schopenhauer 1854 with only the first opera composed; "as only a Frenchman could" glossed (1789). Dresden, Valhalla, Rhine, Paris 1848 deliberately not named. Same ending, changed meaning: "dawn of the golden age, twilight of the Gods of old morality" vs a world giving itself up. The *Ring* from revolution ("old contracts", Siegfried) to Schopenhauer ("versify the fourth book"), "Indian Circe" = Buddhist nothingness; "philosopher of decadence … artist of decadence"; linked to BT 7's "Buddhistic negation of the will" and passive nihilism's weary No — ### Section 4
- CW 5: "Is Wagner a man at all? … a disease"; "That which is harmful lures the exhausted: cabbage lures the vegetarian" (the sick prescribe the régime that keeps them sick); A 6 decadence applied to an audience; "Illness itself can be a stimulus … healthy enough" = boundary, squares diagnosis with EH gratitude; "Wagner est une névrose", Cagliostro (glossed), "three great stimulants of exhausted people: brutality, artificiality and innocence (idiocy)", "success with nerves, and therefore with women", "Only with morbid music can money be made"; clinical words = 1880s symptom-readings; = 9[35]'s aesthetic disguise of passive nihilism — ### Section 5
- CW 6–7: "Above all, no thoughts!", "melody is immoral. Proof: Palestrina. Application: Parsifal"; CW 7 quoted German + English (life no longer animates the whole; word, sentence, page; "anarchy among the atoms, disaggregation of the will … 'freedom of the individual' … 'equal rights for all'"); worked case: writer in love with his phrases; Wagner "our greatest musical miniaturist", fragments of 5–15 bars; large forms do not grow: "disguised his inability to create organic forms, under the cloak of a principle" = his "dramatic style" (stage action decides what comes next); same shape at three scales (figure style-decadence.svg); KeyFact: decadence = structural fact (part overruns whole), checkable without the author's health — ### Sections 6–7
- CW 8–12 in order: the actor; Talma's rule (glossed) "everything which has to strike people as true, must not be true"; "Wagner's music is never true.—But it is supposed to be so"; plots built from effect (CW 9, "begins with the third act"); a calculated effect works only on those who take it for real → needs an audience that does not judge → GS 368 theatre list ("herd, female, pharisee, voting cattle, democrat"), crowd overrides single judgment; Apollonian illusion known as illusion vs actor's illusion that must not be known (needs the herd); CW 11 "an obedient man with long legs" + the Empire = the herd his stage found; CW 12's three demands quoted last — ### Sections 8–12
- Postscripts: refrain "One pays dearly…"; "impossible to refute an illness" = discrediting applied to a taste; Munich wreath "Salvation to the Saviour" → "Salvation from the Saviour" (= *Parsifal*'s last words, *Erlösung dem Erlöser*); *Parsifal* holds the whole diagnosis at once (premise 2, shadow of God, passive nihilism's "nihilistic (Buddhistic) instinct"); passage quoted ("flatters every nihilistic (Buddhistic) instinct", "counterfeit coinage of the transcendental and of a Beyond" = nihilism premise 2, "I admire this work … beauty and disease are united"); Second Postscript: "Decay is universal … not its cause", "ingenuousness of decadence … He believed in it"; Brahms "thirsts after abundance" = GS 370 applied; Wagner the most complete case, not the worst; Bernini in a clause (17th-c. sculptor of marble in theatrical motion) (figure parsifal-grail-temple.jpg, set designer Paul von Joukowsky glossed in caption) — ### The postscripts
- Epilogue: ascending vs declining ages; decadent vs classical aesthetic; master-morality ("symbolic speech of … ascending life, and of the Will to Power") vs Christian; "'World' is a Christian term of abuse"; optics "both necessary", "impossible to refute a diseased eyesight", "true/untrue" senseless in optics; the charge = falsity/duplicity (Icelandic-saga heroes set to the gospel of the lowly); "need of salvation … most honest expression of decadence" (German *ehrlichste*, Common revised); "says yea and nay in one breath … we are false"; "inspired by gratitude"; "Master-morality" glossed (valuation of the strong), used not argued — ### The Epilogue
- Objection, two charges (1: genetic fallacy about the composer's nerves + GM III 4 "separate an artist from his work"; 2: the Epilogue's two "optics of values" are "both necessary", true/untrue senseless) — ## The strongest objection
- ### The first charge: a fallacy about origins: evidence lies in the works (most of the seven plots, 4, 5, 7, 9); GM III 4 read on assigns origin to "physiologists and vivisectionists of the spirit"; discredits not refutes: CW calls the music false (CW 8, CW 12) only as an actor's effect passing for what it is not, exposed by explaining the effect; target = listeners' conviction that being overwhelmed proves depth; two discrediting steps (no independent support; rapture fully explained by stimulants CW 5 and calculation CW 8–9); beauty left standing
- ### Same origin, opposite verdicts: Preface gives the author Wagner's origin and the opposite verdict, so CW judges what each does with the condition, not the source
- ### The second charge: optics: *optics of values* glossed = the angle from which a kind of life sees what is valuable (declining → Christian, ascending → noble), neither argued out of what it sees. Falsity charge survives (duplicity = two optics passing for one; checked by comparing the work with itself; the consistent Christian acquitted). Decadence charge does not: it ranks one optic from inside the other ("diseased eyesight" is diseased only by ascending life's measure; the declining optic, for which holiness is "the only remaining higher value", finds the works its best art)
- ### The gap: the ascending/declining standard (and NcW's "aesthetics … applied physiology") is exactly what the optics passage puts in doubt; asserted, not argued; needs drive psychology
- L16 application, stated once in the <Conclusion> (no separate section): diagnosis built for beliefs meets an art that asserts nothing → reads what a work supplies, discredits instead of refuting; uses shadow of God, decadence, passive nihilism; departures: diagnostician a former patient who loves the drug, standard reaches past the module. Conclusion hands "disaggregation of the will" to self-as-society-of-drives

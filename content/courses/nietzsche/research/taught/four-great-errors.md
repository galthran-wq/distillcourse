# Taught: four-great-errors

## Problem
Opening (= course.yaml `opening`, verbatim): eight sections of TI turn the drive psychology on morality and religion and find four mistakes about cause and effect. Developed as: a moral sentence seems to be a command, not a causal claim, yet section 1 says "every single sentence which religion and morality formulate contains" the confusion of cause and effect; so morality carries a hidden theory of how people work, testable against the module's psychology. Task set: for each error find the module result that exposes it, and mark where the chapter claims more than the module established. Answer: read in order 1–2 / 3 / 4–6 / 7 / 8; three departures (peoples and parties; "no mental causes at all"; "we redeem the world"); section 7's origin story cleared as a discrediting, not a departure; objection (self-undermining + genetic fallacy) answered from section 3's targets and GS 345.

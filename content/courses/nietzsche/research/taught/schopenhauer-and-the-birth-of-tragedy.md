# Taught: schopenhauer-and-the-birth-of-tragedy

## Problem
How can the young Nietzsche accept every premise of Schopenhauer's argument (life swings between pain and boredom) and refuse its conclusion (better not to live)? Answer: the verdict needs an unstated standard (a life judged by what it feels like to live it); BT keeps the description and replaces the standard with an aesthetic one (existence justified as an aesthetic phenomenon), resting on an unargued metaphysical assumption (BT 4) that 1886 discards.

## Explained
- Kant in one paragraph (space/time/cause imposed by the mind; appearance vs thing in itself); Schopenhauer: body known from inside as will → will = inner nature of everything, blind, one; *principium individuationis* = principle of individuation (space/time split one will into many, only in appearance) — ### The world from inside: one blind will
- Will to live (concept will-to-live), Remark; contrast with Plato's good, rational true world — same subsection
- Pendulum, numbered steps: 1 one blind will; 2 want = lack = pain (thirst); 3 satisfaction only removes pain (load-bearing; WWR I §58 "always negative only"); 4 nothing to want → boredom; 5 new want, life swings; WWR I §57 quoted (Payne) — ### The pendulum (FigureSequence pendulum-1..4, frames labelled 2 lack / 3 satisfaction / 4 boredom / 5 new want; box: step 3 carries everything)
- Verdict (step 6) + cure: art = brief will-less release; compassion and asceticism = lasting denial; tragedy → resignation (WWR II ch. 37 quoted via Self-Crit 6). Schopenhauer's pessimism = verdict on value of existence, not a mood (concept schopenhauers-pessimism) — ### The verdict, and the way out
- KeyFact: description → verdict needs a standard (life judged by what it feels like to the one living it); BT replaces the standard — same subsection
- Silenus (Midas legend, Phrygia), BT 3 quoted German + English; Greeks knew (Prometheus, Oedipus, Atreus); "Greek cheerfulness" image reversed — ## Silenus / ### The wisdom of Silenus
- Olympians as transfiguring mirror; "to be able to live… had to create these gods"; theodicy defined, reversed ("only satisfactory theodicy"); Homeric reversal of Silenus (worst: to die soon); Greeks = counterexample to the inference — ### The Olympians as a mirror
- KeyFact: BT reads art as answer to a need (BT 25 "what sufferings this people must have undergone…") → forward to genealogy-as-method
- Apollonian (dream, image, measure, individual, illusion known as such, "know thyself", Apollo = image of principium individuationis) vs Dionysian (intoxication, music, veil of Maya torn — Maya glossed — individual dissolved, "man… has become a work of art"; music copies the will itself, because it has no images and so copies no appearance, only the rise/tension/release of striving; still heard will-lessly, hence also Schopenhauer's release) (concept apollonian-and-dionysian) — ## Apollo and Dionysus (figure apollo-dionysus.svg)

# Taught: decline-of-regimes

## Problem
How does the best city decay, regime by regime, into tyranny, and is the chain a law or a warning? Answer reached: change starts inside the ruling class (mistimed births, mixed metals); each regime honours one good, its excess breeds the son who ends it (honour → wealth → freedom → one lawless desire); the tyrant is the most enslaved man (Thrasymachus answered by a portrait, not a proof). As history the sequence fails (Aristotle, Pol. V.12); as a psychology of regimes it stands (Aristotle keeps it, V.9). The Laws' second telling: two mother forms (Persia/despotism, Athens/freedom), cure = mixture (Sparta).

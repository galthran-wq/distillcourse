# Taught: the-doctrine

## Problem
A position on who rules, who may join and what is owed, stated as a list of conclusions, hides which premise each needs and what would overturn it. Answer: set it out as 17 graded postulates (P1–P17; 15 on the record, 2 argued) and 9 theses (T1–T9) each derived from named postulates/verdicts and no firmer than its weakest premise; then what it allows/forbids, 3 hard cases (all open), 6 critics, 5 open questions. Overall grade on balance, because P8 is.

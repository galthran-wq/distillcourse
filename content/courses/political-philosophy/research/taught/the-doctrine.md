# Taught: the-doctrine

## Problem
A position on who rules, who may join and what is owed, stated as a list of conclusions, hides which premise each needs and what would overturn it. Answer: 17 graded postulates (P1–P17; 15 on the record, 2 argued; P13 a background premise feeding no thesis) plus two common-ground value premises, and 9 theses (T1–T9) each derived from named postulates/verdicts and no firmer than its weakest premise; then 7 permissions / 9 prohibitions, 3 hard cases (open), 6 critics, 5 open questions. Two theses strong (T4, T7), seven on balance; each of the seven rests on at least one on-balance premise (P8 under T2, T3, T6; P4/P6 under T1, T2, T5, T9; Miller under T3; P15 under T8; the floor under T5). P8 is the one whose deciding evidence (trust-and-transfers series) is named and not yet gathered.

# Taught: thrasymachus-and-the-ring

## Problem
If nobody could ever find out, would anyone have a reason to be just? (Glaucon's ring.) Answer reached: Thrasymachus (justice = interest of the ruling body, in every regime) and Glaucon (justice = bargain of the weak, kept from fear) state the challenge; Socrates's Book I replies win exchanges without saying what justice is; Glaucon's three classes of goods show that any defence by rewards concedes the case, so the only answer left is what justice does in the soul — which Socrates proposes to read "in large letters" in a city. Thrasymachus explicitly NOT refuted.

## Explained
- Opening history of the question: laws "in the middle" (Ref greek-miracle); Athens's envoys at Melos, "the strong do what they can and the weak suffer what they must" (one clause, Ref thucydides-power-and-justice#melos-justice-only-between-equals-in-power); if true between cities, perhaps inside one — intro
- Plato (c. 428–348), dialogues; Socrates (469–399), executed by the democracy 399; Republic c. 380, set during the war with Sparta (no exact year) at the Piraeus, house of Cephalus (rich foreigner, shield factory); Glaucon = Plato's brother — intro (Adeimantus, Plato's other brother, introduced where he first speaks, ### What parents and poets praise)
- Polemarchus's "are you stronger than all these? … can you persuade us if we refuse to listen?" as the problem in miniature (force vs persuasion) — intro
- Ring of Gyges (Lydia, western Anatolia; Croesus its last king; Gyges founder of his house), quoted in full; collet glossed (setting holding the stone; turned in = invisible, out = visible: power at will); two rings = controlled test (only detection removed); convergence "at the same point"; just "of necessity"; the honest ring-wearer called "wretched idiot", praised to his face from fear — not merely "people cheat unwatched" — "The ring of Gyges: justice with no witnesses" (### What the test holds fixed / What the test does not say)
- Refutation by single counterexample: Cephalus's "truth + paying debts", the madman's sword; why one case suffices (a definition must cover every case) — "One counterexample kills a definition" / ### The returned sword
- Polemarchus/Simonides "give each his due" = help friends, harm enemies; three refutations (useless: every active use has its own expert, so justice is left only the idle deposit, useful for things while useless; keeper = thief, as the doctor who can prevent a disease can cause it; friends misjudged; harming makes men worse, justice cannot); saying attributed to a "rich and mighty man" (Periander of Corinth c. 600, Xerxes 480) — ### Help friends, harm enemies
- sophist (travelling paid teacher of persuasion); Thrasymachus of Chalcedon (Bosporus); the "twelve" analogy (forbidding the true answer) — "Thrasymachus: the law is what the rulers' interest says it is" / ### An answer forbidden in advance
- Thrasymachus's thesis + regime passage quoted (338d–339a); three steps; "theory of all law", democracy included; Thrasymachus vs the Melos envoys: envoys speak between cities with no common law and say the question of justice does not arise between unequals (suspend justice where there is no law); Thrasymachus speaks of the law inside every city, the question arises and is answered by the stronger part's interest (reduces justice to law) — same section (Ref thucydides#what-the-thesis-does-not-say). Verdict recall (one sentence, no re-telling): between cities the envoys' thesis described the war and failed as a rule (404: Athenians expected Melos's fate, Sparta spared them for Persian-war service; Ref thucydides#the-thesis-describes-the-war-and-fails-as-a-rule-the-record-of-404); Thrasymachus's version is about the inside of a city, which that record does not touch. Intro phrased accordingly ("if that describes how the strong treat the weak there…"). Anacharsis no longer used.
- Rulers err → justice harms the stronger; Cleitophon's "what the ruler thinks" refused; ruler "in the strict sense" never errs; price: ruling is a craft (techne); KeyFact: ruling as craft ⇒ claim to rule = claim to know, "who should rule?" ⇒ "who knows how?" (fwd Ref kallipolis-and-the-philosopher-king) — ### Rulers make mistakes
- Craft argument (a craft lacks nothing, so has no interest of its own and serves only its object: physician/body, pilot/sailors) ⇒ strict ruler serves ruled; shepherd speech quoted; ledger of just man's losses (partnership, tax, office, friends); tyranny passage quoted; three reasons the speech is strong — "Whom does a craft serve?"
- Wage-earning as separate craft; rulers paid by money, honour or penalty; good men rule to avoid being ruled by worse; city of good men would compete to avoid office; shepherd speech framed as counterexample to the craft argument; wage point DEFENDS the craft argument against it (profit belongs to wage-earning) but leaves standing the description of what actual rulers do (both true ⇒ no actual city has a ruler in the strict sense); Laws IV 715b "not polities but parties" quoted (verdict on such cities, not denial they exist); fwd Ref aristotle-regimes-and-the-middle (regimes sorted by common good vs rulers' good); Socrates shifts to "unjust life better" — ### Socrates's answer, and what it leaves standing
- Justice = "sublime simplicity", injustice = "discretion" (blocks shame-based replies) — "Three ways injustice defeats itself"
- pleonexia (urge to have more than one's share); musician/physician argument; concession: justice is virtue and wisdom, injustice vice and ignorance; blush; weakness: assumes a correct measure of wealth/power — ### Wanting more than one's share
- Robber band (Example, 3 steps): "remnant of justice", "half-villains"; extends to city/army/family/single soul; does not show the just man happier than a gang leader — ### Honour among thieves
- ergon (work) / arete (excellence, usually translated "virtue"): pruning hook, eye; soul's work = manage, command, deliberate, live; soul's excellence = justice via the pleonexia concession (justice is virtue); "epicure" confession quoted — ### What a thing is for
- Three classes of goods (for itself / itself + results / results only); many put justice in 3rd, Socrates claims 2nd, "the highest" because valued for itself AND for results; Glaucon's three claims (justice a compromise of the weak; practised unwillingly, of necessity; unjust life better) — "Glaucon restarts the challenge" / ### Three kinds of good
- Glaucon's contract origin quoted (358e–359b): justice = compromise, lesser evil; *contract theory* defined (justice exists because people agreed to it, from weakness); fwd Ref hobbes-state-of-nature-and-covenant; ring = proof of the second claim — ### A bargain among the weak
- L13 refutation: Thrasymachus (strong make law; justice = another's good) vs Glaucon (weak make law; strong man would be mad to sign); shared premise (by nature everyone wants more; restraint only from inability); backward clause: Melos heard two pieces — envoys' "necessary law of their nature" (rule wherever they can) = the shared premise stated between cities, not Thrasymachus's genealogy (envoys appeal to nature where no law exists; Thrasymachus argues from what laws inside a city do); Melians' "common protection" plea = Glaucon's bargain argued by the weaker party (Ref thucydides#the-melians-replies); discriminating test: tax burdening poor vs law against tyranny — ### Two genealogies, not one
- Two statues = setup for the third claim (being × seeming 2×2): perfectly unjust seeming just vs perfectly just seeming unjust (racked, impaled); Aeschylus "to be and not to seem good" — ### Two statues
- Adeimantus: parents/poets/priests praise wages; priests sell absolution; "fox" behind a facade; the demand quoted (367e "whether seen or unseen by gods and men") — ### What parents and poets praise
- Why rewards cannot answer: reward-defence = 3rd-class placement; statues remove rewards by construction; gods bribable per poets — ### Why rewards cannot answer the ring
- Large letters: short-sighted reader; city before soul; build the city from need ("no one is self-sufficing") — "Reading justice in large letters"

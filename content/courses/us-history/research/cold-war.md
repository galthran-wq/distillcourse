# Research brief: cold-war (The Cold War, 1945-1953)

status: planned
module: postwar-america
type: core
goal: Explain containment abroad and anticommunism at home in the first Cold War decade.

THESIS ROLE. The postwar order the 1945 settlement built, run from
Washington: containment, the Marshall Plan, and NATO as the institutional
export of American power — the world-order reset of world-war-two made
permanent. The domestic loyalty state stays in the same frame as the
documented cost, with McCarthy's political (not judicial) fall as the
boundary-enforcement case the course's institutions thread records honestly:
the check that worked was the Senate, late.

## Sources to consult

- **American Yawp Ch.25 "The Cold War"** — containment, Korea, McCarthyism,
  nuclear anxiety.
- **Give Me Liberty Ch.23 "The United States and the Cold War, 1945-1953"** —
  including Nixon's "What Freedom Means to Us" as the Cold War's freedom
  definition, and the civil-liberties cost.
- **Kennedy, *Freedom from Fear*, Epilogue "The World the War Made"** — how
  wartime improvisations (Bretton Woods, the UN, standing military-industrial
  capacity) hardened into the permanent national-security state.
- **MIT 21H.102 Session 13-14** — "The United States and the Creation of Three
  Worlds" (Cold War origins, decolonization, the American Century) and Cold War
  culture, domesticity, and the Lavender Scare.
- **Milestone Documents** #75 UN Charter, #77 Truman Doctrine, #78 Marshall
  Plan, #79 Recognition of Israel, #80 EO 9981, #81 Korean armistice,
  #82 Senate Resolution 301 censuring McCarthy.

## Key points the lesson must cover

1. **Why the alliance broke.** Competing security requirements in eastern Europe,
   the atomic monopoly and its brevity, economic reconstruction models, and
   mutual misreading. Present the standard interpretive positions (orthodox,
   revisionist, post-revisionist) in three lines each and say which evidence
   each rests on — this is the course's clearest place to show that causal
   attribution in recent history is contested.
2. **Containment as doctrine and as instruments.** Kennan's Long Telegram and
   "X" article; the Truman Doctrine (1947) generalizing aid to Greece and Turkey
   into a global commitment; the Marshall Plan (1948) as economic containment
   and as a market-building program; NATO (1949); NSC-68 (1950) converting
   containment into a military-budget program.
3. **1949-1950 changed the temperature.** The Soviet atomic test, the Chinese
   Communist victory, and then Korea. The Korean War (1950-53): UN authorization
   in the USSR's absence, Inchon, Chinese intervention, MacArthur's dismissal,
   stalemate at roughly the prewar line, about 36,000 US dead, and an armistice
   that still holds without a peace treaty. Korea militarized containment
   permanently — defense spending roughly tripled and did not come back down.
4. **The domestic loyalty state.** Truman's Loyalty Program (1947), HUAC and the
   Hollywood blacklist, the Smith Act prosecutions, the Hiss and Rosenberg
   cases, McCarthy from 1950, and the Lavender Scare's purge of gay and lesbian
   federal employees (a larger purge by headcount than the communist one).
5. **The limits.** McCarthy's fall — the Army-McCarthy hearings and Senate
   censure (Resolution 301, 1954) — showing that the boundary was enforced
   politically rather than judicially. The Supreme Court largely deferred during
   the peak years.
6. **Cold War and civil rights, linked.** Executive Order 9981 desegregating the
   armed forces (1948); the State Department's concern that segregation was a
   propaganda liability (Dudziak) — a mechanism the affluent-society and
   civil-rights lessons build on.

## Set pieces and worked examples to recreate

- **The Truman Doctrine speech read as a rhetorical move**: how a specific aid
  request for two countries was framed as a universal commitment to "free
  peoples". Worked example: identify the specific ask and the general principle,
  and state what the generalization licensed. Parallel exercise on NSC-68's
  threat framing.
- **Marshall Plan arithmetic**: dollars per recipient country, share of European
  GDP, and the political condition attached (participation required accepting a
  market-integration framework, which is why the USSR refused for its bloc).
- **The loyalty-program tally**: employees investigated, dismissals, and
  resignations — the ratio is the point.
- **Korea's line**: a map showing the front moving four times and ending where it
  started.

## Pitfalls / subtleties to flag

- Do not present the Cold War as inevitable or as a simple case of one side's
  aggression; state the interpretive dispute and the evidence.
- McCarthy did not create the loyalty apparatus; it predates him by three years
  and outlasted him. He is a symptom worth naming, not the cause.
- Some accusations were true (Soviet espionage in the 1930s-40s is documented in
  the Venona decrypts); the point about McCarthyism is the method — accusation
  without evidence, guilt by association, and the destruction of careers without
  process.
- The Korean War is not a footnote: it killed more Koreans proportionally than
  almost any modern conflict and set the permanent US military posture.
- Decolonization is happening simultaneously and gets read through the Cold War
  lens by US policymakers, with consequences the sixties lesson inherits.
